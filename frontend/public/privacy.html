Privacy Policy

Effective November 19, 2026

Information we collect

We collect account and company details, authentication and security records, subscription identifiers, support communications, project names and measurements, property addresses and boundaries, uploaded plans or images, and technical records such as IP address, browser details, request identifiers, and error logs.

How we use information

We use information to provide and secure workspaces, process subscriptions, complete managed takeoffs, communicate service events, prevent abuse, troubleshoot errors, maintain backups, and comply with legal obligations. We do not sell customer project content or personal information.

Service providers

We use infrastructure, payment, mapping, imagery, email, monitoring, and security providers only as needed to operate the service. Stripe processes payment-card information; McKenna does not store complete card numbers. Mapping providers receive technical requests needed to display the maps their terms permit.

Retention and deletion

Active workspace data is retained while the account is in use. Security and billing records may be retained for legitimate fraud, tax, accounting, and legal needs. Deleted content may remain in encrypted backups until the applicable backup cycle expires. Customers can request account export or deletion through support.

Security

We use access controls, encrypted transport, private storage, hashed passwords, monitoring, and backups. No system can guarantee absolute security. Report suspected incidents promptly.

Your choices

You may update account information, cancel billing, request a copy of account data, or request deletion, subject to necessary legal and billing retention. Marketing messages, if introduced, will include an opt-out; essential security and transaction messages cannot be disabled while an account remains active.
